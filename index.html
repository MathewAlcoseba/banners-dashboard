Paste the published CSV URL of your mirror sheet's [PROMOS] CMS & Banners tab. The dashboard will pull live data and show every promo with a deadline that hasn't yet passed.

In your mirror sheet, go to File → Share → Publish to web, pick the tab, choose CSV, click Publish.
ii.
Copy the URL that ends in &output=csv and paste it above.
iii.
Hit Connect. Nothing writes back to the sheet — this is strictly read-only.
